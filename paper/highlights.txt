Highlights

• First Python framework providing native spatial cross-validation for geoscience applications

• Standard CV underestimates error by 5--31% across environmental and mineral exploration domains

• Validated across two case studies with different spatial autocorrelation strengths (I = 0.336--0.545)

• Comparison with Kriging demonstrates complementary strengths of ML and geostatistical approaches

• Provides practical guidelines: use spatial CV when Moran's I > 0.3
